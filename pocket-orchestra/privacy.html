Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

Overview

Pocket Orchestra's instruments and saved performances work without an account or wallet. The app has no advertising service, analytics service, or remote application server. An optional wallet connection is available in Android versions that include this feature, as described below.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your saved performances and settings in its private local application storage. A saved performance contains instrument actions and their timing so the app can play the performance again. Pocket Orchestra does not record microphone audio. This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to fadezdev.

Optional wallet connection

If you choose Connect wallet, Pocket Orchestra opens a compatible wallet app and requests authorization and a signed sign-in message. Your wallet receives Pocket Orchestra's name, website, icon, network identifier, and a one-time sign-in request. Pocket Orchestra receives your public address, an optional account label, the signed message and signature, and an authorization token. The app verifies the signature locally and holds the connected address and authorization in memory for the current app session. It does not save these details with your music, send them to a developer server, or use them for analytics.

Connecting does not request a payment, create a blockchain transaction, provide cloud backup, or give the app your private keys or recovery phrase. Disconnect wallet clears the connection on this device and asks your wallet to revoke its permission. If that request cannot finish, you can remove Pocket Orchestra in your wallet's connected-app settings. Closing the app process also clears its in-memory connection; the wallet may separately retain permission until revoked.

Your chosen wallet may use network services to verify the app's website and signing identity and handles its own data under its privacy policy. Wallet use is optional, and all instruments and saved plays remain available without it. fadezdev does not sell, rent, or transmit your wallet data to a developer server.

Audio and network access

Instrument sounds are included with the app and can be used offline after installation. The app does not upload performances or audio. Wallet connection may require internet access for your wallet's checks. Opening the Privacy Policy or Terms & Conditions from the app opens this website in your browser; see the website information below.

Permissions

Pocket Orchestra does not request microphone, camera, contacts, or location permissions. Android may expose system-level audio controls needed to play sound through the device. Wallet authorization is requested only when you choose to connect and is handled by your wallet app.

Children’s privacy

The music features do not require personal information. Children should use the instruments without connecting a wallet. A parent or guardian can remove locally saved performances by deleting them inside the app or uninstalling the app. Wallet providers have their own eligibility requirements.

This website

This informational website does not use advertising, analytics, account cookies, or contact forms. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process basic request information under GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

Changes

If Pocket Orchestra’s data practices change, this policy will be updated and the effective date above will be revised.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be submitted through the developer’s public GitHub profile at github.com/fadezdev.
