Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

Overview

Pocket Orchestra is designed to work without an account, wallet, advertising service, analytics service, or remote application server. fadezdev does not collect, sell, rent, or share personal information through the Pocket Orchestra app.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your saved performances and settings in its private local application storage. A saved performance contains instrument actions and their timing so the app can play the performance again. Pocket Orchestra does not record microphone audio. This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to fadezdev.

Motion sensor use

The Maracas instrument uses the device accelerometer while the Maracas screen is open. Motion samples are processed immediately on the device to detect a shake. Raw motion data is not saved, linked to your identity, or transmitted elsewhere. Sensor listening stops when you leave the Maracas screen.

Audio and network access

Instrument sounds are included with the app and can be used offline after installation. The app does not upload performances or audio. Future versions that introduce an online feature will update this policy before that feature is released.

Permissions

Pocket Orchestra does not request microphone, camera, contacts, location, or wallet permissions. Android may expose system-level audio controls needed to play sound through the device.

Children’s privacy

Pocket Orchestra does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users. A parent or guardian can remove locally saved performances by deleting them inside the app or uninstalling the app.

This website

This informational website does not use advertising, analytics, account cookies, or contact forms. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process basic request information under GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

Changes

If Pocket Orchestra’s data practices change, this policy will be updated and the effective date above will be revised.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be submitted through the developer’s public GitHub profile at github.com/fadezdev.
